Lanka Ride — what each member built

Group 2026-Y2-S1-MLB-B13G2-05 · SE2030 · Spring Boot 3.3, JSP, Spring Security, MySQL lanka_ride

Lanka Ride is a vehicle rental web application. A customer searches a branch, asks to book a car, a supervisor approves or denies the request, finance takes payment, and staff record pickup and return. Fleet staff can take a car offline for maintenance. Support staff answer tickets. Managers read reports.

Every feature uses the same path. The browser shows a JSP page. The form posts to a controller. The controller calls a service. The service checks the rules and asks a repository to read or write MySQL. The controller then redirects, and the next JSP draws the result. A hidden CSRF token sits on every POST form. This guide does not change any of that Java.

header.jsp and footer.jsp wrap every screen. The header chooses a marketing bar on the home page, a short Lanka Pay bar on the card page, or the signed-in menu everywhere else. sec:authorize hides links the current role must not see. app.css is the only stylesheet. Photos sit behind the home page, login, register, the customer dashboard, and the maintenance and admin screens. The forms and numbers stay in white cards.

1. Sakalasooriya S.A.A.A. — vehicle management only

IT25102266 owns vehicle management and nothing else in this guide. That is the car catalogue: search, the detail page, add, edit, transfer between branches, retire, and permanent delete. A visitor can search cars without signing in. Creating, editing, transferring, retiring, or deleting a car needs ADMIN, FLEET_COORDINATOR, or OPERATIONS_MANAGER. Branches, staff accounts, login, and support tickets belong to other members.

CRUD

ActionURLWhat it does
ReadGET /vehiclesSearch by branch, category, dates, and “available only”. Retired cars stay hidden.
ReadGET /vehicles/{id}One car: price, seats, photo, branch, status, transfer history.
CreateGET/POST /vehicles/new and POST /vehiclesSave a new car on a branch. Registration number must be unique.
UpdateGET/POST /vehicles/{id}/edit and POST /vehicles/{id}Change the same car. The plate check ignores its own row.
UpdatePOST /vehicles/{id}/transferMove the car to another branch and write a branch_transfers row.
UpdatePOST /vehicles/{id}/retireStatus becomes RETIRED. The row stays in the database.
DeletePOST /vehicles/{id}/deletePermanent delete. Payments, bookings, maintenance, and transfers for that car are removed first.

Screens

  • vehicle/list.jsp — filter form and a card for each car. Search is a GET, so the address bar holds the filters.
  • vehicle/detail.jsp — photo, specs, and the staff buttons for edit, transfer, retire, and delete.
  • vehicle/form.jsp — Spring form:form bound to a Vehicle. The same page creates and edits. A small script fills brand and model from the saved fleet defaults. Those defaults are Wickramasinghe’s catalogue, not this module.

Java, in plain words

  • Vehicle is the table vehicles. Status is AVAILABLE, UNAVAILABLE, MAINTENANCE, or RETIRED. A car belongs to one Branch.
  • VehicleRepository.search is a JPQL query. Blank filters are ignored. It never returns RETIRED cars.
  • VehicleService.search runs that query, then drops cars whose bookings overlap the chosen dates. Pending, approved, and ongoing bookings block the car.
  • VehicleService.create and update clean text, check the plate, check seats from 1 to 20, and check money through InputChecks.
  • VehicleService.transfer refuses a retired car and a move to the same branch. It saves a BranchTransfer with who did it and when.
  • VehicleController is thin. It reads the form, calls the service, and redirects with a flash message. That is the post-redirect-get pattern, so a refresh does not save the car twice.
  • His Java files are Vehicle, VehicleController, VehicleService, VehicleRepository, Branch, BranchRepository, BranchTransfer, and BranchTransferRepository. Branch is the depot a car belongs to. Adding a new depot is not this module.

2. De Silva D.L.K.C. — fleet maintenance

He owns service records. Opening a job sets the car to MAINTENANCE so a new booking is refused. Closing the job sets the car back to AVAILABLE. Only ADMIN and FLEET_COORDINATOR can open /maintenance.

CRUD

ActionURLWhat it does
ReadGET /maintenanceList every service record.
ReadGET /maintenance/{id}One record, the car, and the close form.
CreateGET /maintenance/new then POST /maintenanceOpen a job. If the car has a future booking, the form asks the user to confirm.
UpdateGET /maintenance/{id}/edit then POST /maintenance/{id}Edit an open job. A closed job cannot be edited.
UpdatePOST /maintenance/{id}/closeStore the final cost, mark CLOSED, and make the car AVAILABLE again.
DeletePOST /maintenance/{id}/deleteRemove that record.
UpdatePOST /maintenance/remindersSend due-service reminders into the notification log.

Screens

  • fleet/list.jsp — cards for each job, plus “Add record” and “Send due reminders”.
  • fleet/form.jsp — vehicle, service type, dates, cost, description, and mechanics. The same form edits an existing job.
  • fleet/detail.jsp — the job, a close button, and delete. Close posts the final cost.

These three pages use the garage photo behind the white cards.

Java, in plain words

  • MaintenanceRecord stores the service type, dates, costs, description, mechanics, status (OPEN, IN_PROGRESS, CLOSED), and the vehicle.
  • MaintenanceService.create looks for bookings that still need the car. Without the confirm flag it stops and tells the controller to show the warning. With the flag it saves the job and sets the vehicle status to MAINTENANCE.
  • MaintenanceService.close writes the final cost, sets CLOSED, and sets the vehicle back to AVAILABLE.
  • MaintenanceService.openInspectionFromReturn is called from the payment return path when staff record a mileage or damage discrepancy. The booking stays. A new maintenance row is opened.
  • MaintenanceReminderScheduler can call the same reminder method on a timer. The button on the list page calls it immediately.
  • MaintenanceController only gathers form fields, calls the service, and redirects. The conflict warning is an exception turned into a message on the form.

3. Samaranayake P.I.S. — bookings

He is Scrum Master and he owns the reservation. A booking is a request, not an instant rental. It starts PENDING and UNPAID. A supervisor approves or denies it. The customer or staff can cancel while it is still pending or approved. The monitor page is read-only.

CRUD and the status changes

ActionURLWhat it does
ReadGET /bookingsA customer sees only their trips. Staff see every booking.
ReadGET /bookings/pendingStaff queue of PENDING requests.
ReadGET /bookings/monitorOperations view. No buttons that change data.
ReadGET /bookings/{id}One booking. Staff see approve and deny. The customer sees pay when it is approved.
CreateGET /bookings/new then POST /bookingsCustomer asks for a car, a branch, and two dates. Status becomes PENDING.
UpdatePOST /bookings/{id}/approvePENDING becomes APPROVED. Payment status becomes PENDING_PAYMENT.
UpdatePOST /bookings/{id}/denyPENDING becomes DENIED, with a reason.
UpdatePOST /bookings/{id}/cancelCustomer or staff cancel a pending or approved trip.
DeletePOST /bookings/{id}/deletePermanent delete of that booking when the rules allow it.

Pickup and return mileage are stored on the booking, but the buttons live on Kavindi’s invoice page. The two modules share the Booking row.

Screens

  • booking/list.jsp — cards, status pills, and filters for pending or all.
  • booking/form.jsp — dates and the chosen car. The page script stops a return date that is before pickup.
  • booking/detail.jsp — the decision screen. Approve and deny are separate POST forms. The pay link goes to the invoice.
  • booking/monitor.jsp — the same trips in a staff board, without edit buttons.

Java, in plain words

  • Booking holds the customer, vehicle, branch, dates, status, payment status, mileage, fuel, late fee, damage charge, and a discrepancy note.
  • BookingService.create rejects a car that is in maintenance, unavailable, or retired. It then calls countOverlapping. If another pending, approved, or ongoing booking covers those dates, the save is refused.
  • approve only works on PENDING, and it checks the car status again. A car that went into the workshop after the request cannot be approved.
  • deny stores the staff reason. cancel checks that the caller is the customer or a staff member.
  • BookingRepository.countOverlapping is the date rule: pickup is on or before the end, and return is on or after the start, and the status is in the blocking list.
  • BookingController reads the signed-in name and passes it to the service. Customers and staff share the same URLs. The service and @PreAuthorize decide who may approve.

4. Kavindi P.D.N. — payments

She owns the money. Each movement is its own row: rental, deposit, late fee, damage, or refund. The invoice reads those rows. The card number is checked by a sandbox gateway and then thrown away. Only the brand and the last four digits are stored.

CRUD

ActionURLWhat it does
ReadGET /paymentsStaff ledger of every transaction.
ReadGET /payments/booking/{id}The invoice for one booking.
CreateGET/POST /payments/booking/{id}/checkoutLanka Pay. Visa, Mastercard, or American Express. A test card ending 4242 is approved. A card ending 0002 is declined.
ReadGET .../checkout/successReceipt after a successful charge.
UpdatePOST .../pickupStaff record pickup mileage and fuel. Booking becomes ONGOING.
UpdatePOST .../returnStaff record return mileage, fuel, late fee, and damage. Booking becomes COMPLETED. Extra charges become new payment rows.
CreatePOST .../refundFinance or admin refund an allowed booking. A refund row is inserted. The old payment row is not edited.

Screens

  • payment/list.jsp — the ledger cards: type, amount, who recorded it, and success or failure.
  • payment/invoice.jsp — amount due, pay button, pickup form, return form, and refund form. Which block shows depends on the booking status.
  • payment/gateway.jsp — the card form. It uses the short Lanka Pay header.
  • payment/gateway-success.jsp — the approved receipt and the link back to the invoice.

Java, in plain words

  • PaymentTransaction is one line of the ledger: type, amount, gateway reference, brand, last four digits, success flag, and the staff or customer who caused it.
  • DummyGateway.charge checks length, the Luhn digit, the brand, the name, the expiry, and the CVV. It returns a decision. It does not save the full card number.
  • PaymentService.payApprovedBooking only runs when the booking is APPROVED and waiting for payment. A decline does not mark the booking paid.
  • recordPickup stores the mileage and fuel and moves the booking to ONGOING.
  • completeReturn stores return readings, optional late fee and damage, inserts those charges, and marks the booking COMPLETED. A discrepancy note can open a maintenance job through De Silva’s service.
  • refund inserts a refund row and sets the payment status to REFUNDED when the booking is allowed to be refunded.
  • PaymentController passes the signed-in username into the service so the ledger can show who took the payment.

Screens

  • dashboard/customer.jsp — “My trips”. Counts and recent booking cards over the fleet photo.
  • dashboard/reports.jsp — period buttons, branch filter, totals, and a print-friendly table.

Java, in plain words

  • DashboardController.dashboard calls count() and countByStatus on the vehicle repository and countPending on the booking service. It puts those numbers on the model. The JSP only prints them.
  • customerHome loads that customer’s bookings and counts how many are active and how many are approved but unpaid.
  • ReportService.buildReport chooses a date window from “daily”, “monthly”, or “annual”, then sums successful payment rows and counts bookings and vehicles. A branch id narrows the query.
  • ReportService.toCsv turns that same map into text. ReportsController returns it with content type text/csv.
  • FleetDefault is a small lookup row: kind (category, model, or service type) and a label. FleetDefaultService lists, saves, and deletes them. The vehicle form reads the lists so staff do not invent a new spelling of the same model every time.

6. Pahasara Y.A.P. — support and notifications

He owns tickets, the notification log, password reset, the role menus, and the admin screen that adds a branch or a staff user. Email is not sent to a real inbox. Each attempt is a row in the notification log, and a failed row can be retried. Public registration always creates a customer. Only this admin screen can create a staff account.

  • support/form.jsp — subject and message.

  • support/notifications.jsp — channel, recipient, subject, delivery status, and the retry button.

  • auth/login.jsp and auth/register.jsp — sign in and public customer registration. Login posts to Spring Security at /login.

Java, in plain words

  • SupportTicket stores the customer, subject, message, staff reply, status, and timestamps. There is no separate support service class. SupportController talks to SupportTicketRepository directly, and calls NotificationService after a create or a reply.
  • NotificationService.send writes a NotificationLog row. retryFailed walks the failed rows and marks another attempt.
  • PasswordResetController looks up the user by username or email, saves a PasswordResetToken, and puts the link text in the log. The reset method checks the token, encodes the new password, and marks the token used.
  • AdminController lists branches and users, then addBranch saves a Branch and addUser saves a UserAccount with one role. Both posts are ADMIN only.
  • Booking and payment code also call NotificationService when a trip is approved, paid, or updated. The log is the shared record that those events happened.

How to talk about it in the viva

  • Say which URL you would open, which JSP draws it, which controller method receives the POST, and which service method applies the rule.
  • IT25102266: vehicle management only. Search hides retired cars and cars already booked on those dates. Transfer writes a history row. Delete removes the child rows first. Do not present branches, staff users, or login as this member’s use case.
  • Maintenance: open job means the car cannot be booked. Close job means it can.
  • Booking: pending, then approved or denied. Overlap is checked in the repository.
  • Payment: one row per movement. The gateway keeps the last four digits, not the card.
  • Dashboard: counts come from the same tables the other members write. Reports do not store their own totals.
  • Support: a ticket is saved even when email is only a log row. A failed alert stays visible so it can be retried.

The design is MVC. The controller is the front door, the service is the rules, and the repository is the database. Status values are enums with if-checks. They are not a separate state-machine class.
