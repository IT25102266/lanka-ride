Suggested order

  1. Log in as customer and submit a booking from Find a ride.
  2. Log in as supervisor and approve it.
  3. Log in as customer and pay the deposit and rental.
  4. Log in as fleet, record pickup (fuel FULL), then return with fuel EMPTY.
  5. Log in as operations and open Monitor. The booking should be flagged.
  6. Log in as finance and open Payments, then Reports (Print and Download CSV).
  7. Log in as fleet, add or edit a vehicle, transfer it, and open Maintenance.
  8. Log in as customer and open a support ticket. Log in as admin to reply and to open Alerts.
